## ⚖️ Essential Algorithmic Rules & Golden Metrics

### 1. Consequent Encroachment (C.E.) & Candle Mechanics
- **50% Midpoint Rule:** Wicks may penetrate through Consequent Encroachment (C.E.) of an inefficiency or wick, but **candlestick bodies must close respecting the 50% line** to preserve institutional order flow integrity.
- **Immediate Rebalance (IRB):** When the immediately subsequent candle completely fills the gap created by the prior candle—one of the cleanest institutional signatures.

### 2. Multi-Workspace Architecture (Zero Clutter)
Never put all studies on one chart. Maintain separate TradingView layouts:
1. **Execution / Naked Chart:** Short-term intraday delivery & execution.
2. **HTF Key Levels Workspace:** Monthly, Weekly, and Daily PMH/PML/PMC and PWH/PWL.
3. **NWOG / NDOG Workspace:** 5-week rotating New Week and New Day Opening Gaps.
4. **RTH Opening Range Gap Workspace:** Settlement gap matrices, gradients & standard deviations.
5. **First Presented FVG Workspace:** Intraday initial displacement tracking.

### 3. Economic Calendar & Non-Farm Payroll (NFP) Discipline
- **NFP Monday:** The premier high-probability trading day of NFP week (institutions position early).
- **NFP Wednesday:** **Hard cutoff at 11:00 AM EST**—cease all trading activity.
- **NFP Thursday & Friday:** Strict no-touch observation zone.

### 4. Backtesting Standard: Screen Recording over Market Replay
- Market replay is unrealistic because it skips the true "breath and lifespan" of candles and hides emotional drawdown ("heat").
- Use Windows native screen recording (**`Alt + Windows + R`**) on free/delayed data (15-min delay is identical to live data for backtesting).
- Practice the **Pause-Predict-Unpause drill** with vocal commentary.
