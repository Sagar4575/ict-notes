## 1. Onboarding Philosophy & Student Progression Plan

### 1.1 Overcoming Library Intimidation
- **The Problem:** New students are often overwhelmed by the hundreds of videos in the ICT YouTube library, leading to "Netflix & chill" passive watching or trying to rush through content too quickly without retaining anything.
- **The Solution:** Strip away ambiguity and focus on a daily, structured routine of recording three daily repeating algorithmic phenomena.
- **Asset Focus:** Use **US Index Futures (NASDAQ / NQ)** on 1-minute charts. Futures are regulated (non-CFD) and provide consistent algorithmic price delivery. The same principles apply to Forex, Stocks, and CFDs.

### 1.2 The 6-Week Onboarding Progression Strategy
Do not attempt to hunt for all three phenomena on Day 1. Follow a phased approach to build pattern-recognition habits:
* **Weeks 1–2:** Focus exclusively on **Pillar 1** (Pre-Market Hours & Liquidity Pools).
* **Weeks 3–4:** Add **Pillar 2** (RTH Opening Range Gap & Consequent Encroachment).
* **Weeks 5–6:** Add **Pillar 3** (Post-10:00 AM Silver Bullet First Presented FVG).
* **Advanced Students:** If already familiar with ICT concepts but lacking consistency, skip the phased rollout and begin logging all three pillars immediately.

## 2. Pillar 1: Pre-Market Hours Phase (07:00 AM – 09:00 AM EST)

### 2.1 Chart Setup & Window Framing
1. Draw two vertical lines on the 1-minute chart at **07:00 AM EST** and **09:00 AM EST**.
2. Identify the **Highest High** and **Lowest Low** established strictly within this 2-hour window.
3. Annotate any **Relative Equal Highs (EQH)** or **Relative Equal Lows (EQL)** inside the window.

### 2.2 Market Phase / Condition Classification
Classify the pre-market price action into one of two primary market conditions:
* **Trending / Expansion:** Price makes a continuous directional run from one end of the 7–9 AM window to the other.
* **Consolidation / Range-Bound:** Price oscillates sideways within a tight range.

### 2.3 Post-09:30 AM RTH Behavioral Relationship ("The Crystal Ball")
* Pre-market behavior acts as a precursor for how price will deliver after the **09:30 AM EST Regular Trading Hours (RTH)** open.
* **Consolidation Pre-Market:** High probability of a post-9:30 AM expansion run that targets the pre-market EQH/EQL or Highest High / Lowest Low liquidity pools.
* **Trending Pre-Market:** Post-9:30 AM may expand initially to sweep a liquidity pool, followed by afternoon consolidation.

### 2.4 Pillar 1 Logging Checklist
* Record raw numbers for Highest High, Lowest Low, EQH, and EQL.
* Log pre-market phase (Trending vs. Consolidation).
* Record time elapsed post-9:30 AM before price reacts off pre-market liquidity pools.

## 3. Pillar 2: Regular Trading Hours Opening Range Gap (RTH ORG) & Consequent Encroachment (C.E.)

### 3.1 Chart Configuration
1. Toggle the TradingView 1-minute chart session setting from **ETH** (Electronic Trading Hours) to **RTH** (Regular Trading Hours) in the lower-right corner.
2. Identify the gap between the previous trading day's **16:14 EST close** (final 1-min print) and the current day's **09:30 EST open**.

### 3.2 Rectangle Framing & Consequent Encroachment (C.E.)
* Draw a rectangle spanning from the 16:14 EST close to the 09:30 EST open.
* Enable the midpoint line setting in the rectangle tool options to mark **Consequent Encroachment (C.E.)**—the 50% midpoint of the gap.

### 3.3 The 70% Midpoint Rule & Execution Targets
* **70% Probability Rule:** There is approximately a **70% statistical likelihood** that price returns to at least **50% of the RTH gap (C.E.) by 10:00 AM EST**.
* **Trading Application:** When hunting short/long setups (e.g., using an Inversion FVG or Bearish Order Block), target the RTH ORG C.E. level. Full gap closure is not required to take profit.

### 3.4 Pillar 2 Logging Checklist
* Record gap size in handles.
* Log whether price reached C.E. by 10:00 AM EST.
* Track minutes required post-09:30 AM for price to reach C.E.

## 4. Pillar 3: Post-10:00 AM Silver Bullet First Presented FVG

### 4.1 Identification Rules
1. Mark the **10:00 AM EST** vertical line on the 1-minute chart.
2. Locate the **very first Fair Value Gap (FVG)** or **Volume Imbalance (VI)** that forms at or immediately after 10:00 AM EST (e.g., the 10:00 or 10:01 AM candle).
3. Draw a rectangle highlighting the FVG (or SIBI/BISI) and extend it to the right.

### 4.2 Entry Delivery & Liquidity Target
* Monitor the price retracement back into this first presented FVG.
* Target short-term highs or lows (buy-side / sell-side liquidity) formed prior to or immediately following the 10:00 AM FVG creation.

### 4.3 Pillar 3 Logging Checklist
* Record exact time of FVG formation (e.g., 10:01 AM EST).
* Measure time (in minutes) from FVG creation to first price entry/retest.
* Record total handle range from entry price to target liquidity point.

## 5. Journaling Protocol & Data Collection Standard

### 5.1 Screenshot Requirements & Formatting
* Take **3 clean screenshots per day** (1 for each pillar).
* **Zoom & Center:** Ensure the chart is zoomed and centered to leave ample whitespace around price candles for clean annotations.
* **Annotation Aesthetics:** Maintain extreme neatness and organization, treating the log entries with professional rigor.

### 5.2 The 12-Week Dataset Standard
* **6 Weeks Historical:** Go back 6 weeks in NASDAQ (NQ) historical data and log all 3 pillars for every trading day.
* **6 Weeks Forward:** Walk forward for 6 weeks, logging all 3 pillars daily (approx. 20 minutes per day).
* **Result:** A 12-week comprehensive dataset proving algorithmic repetition.

### 5.3 Electronic Journaling & Tagging
* Use digital journaling software to tag entries with searchable keywords (e.g., `PreMarket_Consolidation`, `RTH_ORG_CE_Filled`, `SilverBullet_10am_FVG`).
* **Weekend Review Ritual:** Review static charts every Saturday/Sunday without live market noise to identify missed details and reinforce pattern recognition.
