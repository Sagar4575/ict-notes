# ICT Lecture Notes: Part 5 — Backtesting Properly & Subconscious Conditioning

---

## 1. Executive Overview & The Psychology of Backtesting

### The Core Purpose of Backtesting
* **Pseudo-Experience & Neural Conditioning:** Backtesting is not merely a statistical exercise; it is a method for tricking the human subconscious into building "pseudo-experience." By repeatedly observing, annotating, and logging price action delivery, the subconscious records visual patterns and subtle nuances that are later seamlessly retrieved during live market conditions.
* **The 12-Week Dataset Standard:**
  * **6 Weeks Historical (Past Data):** Going back through old data to analyze setups, patterns, and failure points.
  * **6 Weeks Walk-Forward (Future/Current Data):** Recording daily price action each day to test logic in real-time or delayed conditions.
* **Elimination of Over-Complication:** The core Smart Money Concepts (SMC) framework is built on simple, repeatable pillars. Traders do not need overly complex indicators or black-box algorithms; a robust narrative can be constructed entirely around 3 foundational pillars.

## 2. Critique of Market Replay vs. Screen-Recorded Life-Span Analysis

### Why Market Replay is "Toxic" and Unrealistic
* **Loss of Candlestick Lifespan:** Market Replay tools jump from open to high/low to close in a few discrete ticks. They omit the "breath, birth, and death" of individual 1-minute candlesticks.
* **False Psychological Reality:** Replay hides active drawdown ("heat") and time duration. In live trading, a trader must endure minutes of price fluctuating inside a dealing range or FVG. Replay eliminates this psychological conditioning, rendering the trader unprepared for real-time order flow dynamics.
* **The Illusion of Speed:** Higher timeframes (e.g., Weekly or Daily) do not move slower than lower timeframes (e.g., 1-Minute). Side-by-side, price prints simultaneously across all timeframes. The perceived "speed" of lower timeframes is a mental illusion.

### Recommended Screen-Recording Methodology
* **Native Windows Recording Shortcut:**
  * Press **`Alt + Windows Key + R`** to initiate native screen recording in Windows (ensure the microphone is enabled).
* **Delayed Data vs. Live Data:**
  * Free/delayed data (e.g., 15–20 minute delay on TradingView) is completely sufficient for backtesting. Because the data has already occurred, the exact formation, tick movement, and candlestick lifespan remain identical to live feed.
  * Paying for live data feed is unnecessary when studying past price delivery.
* **Active Voice Logging & "Pause-Predict-Unpause" Drills:**
  * Record video logs while narrating observations in your own voice.
  * Pause recorded price action, speak the predicted direction, entry, and stop-loss rules out loud, then unpause to verify how the algorithm delivers price.

## 3. Subconscious Conditioning & Journaling Standards

### "Sugar-Coated Love Letters" to Your Future Self
* **Elimination of Toxic Journaling:** Never record negative, angry, or self-deprecating remarks in your trading journal (e.g., *"I blew the account," "I am stupid," "I lost $25,000"*).
* **Positive Subconscious Reinforcement:**
  * Frame every journal entry with constructive praise of your observation skills.
  * Annotate hindsight examples as if you executed them with perfect precision and discipline.
  * Writing positive reinforcement tricks the subconscious mind into shedding fear, anxiety, and hesitation, accelerating the transition from novice to competent trader.
* **Parenting the "Inner Child" Trader:** Treat your current trading self as an inner child that needs positive guidance and encouragement to mature into a disciplined adult trader.

### Precision & Chart Real Estate Protocol
* **Numerical Precision Rule:**
  * Avoid vague estimates (e.g., *"around 560ish"*).
  * Use TradingView's **Price Note** tool to anchor exact numerical figures (Open, High, Low, Close, C.E.) to key reference points (e.g., `29,562.25`).
  * Sloppy chart logging breeds sloppy execution in live trading.
* **Utilizing Chart Real Estate & Legends:**
  * Use empty whitespace on charts for detailed explanatory notes.
  * When chart real estate is constrained, tag levels with letters (`A`, `B`, `C`) and build a comprehensive explanatory legend in the margin.

## 5. Pillar 1: Pre-Market Hours Analysis (07:00–09:00 AM EST)

### Establishing the Pre-Market Dealing Range
* Mark 07:00 AM EST and 09:00 AM EST with vertical lines.
* Identify the **Highest High** and **Lowest Low** created within this 2-hour window.
* Draw a Fibonacci grid across the high and low, truncating the lines precisely at 09:00 AM EST to prevent visual clutter during Regular Trading Hours (RTH).

### Algorithmic Reference Points
* **Relative Equal Highs (EQH) & Relative Equal Lows (EQL):** Any clean, equal highs or lows formed between 07:00 and 09:00 AM EST act as high-probability liquidity pools that the algorithm will target during the RTH morning session (09:30 AM–11:30 AM EST).
* **Confluence with RTH Levels:** When pre-market Fibonacci levels (e.g., 50% Equilibrium) align with RTH Opening Range Gap levels, those prices become institutional key levels.

## 6. Pillar 2: RTH Opening Range Gap (RTH ORG) & Standard Deviations

### Defining the RTH Opening Range Gap
* **Time Reference Points:**
  * **Settlement Price:** Final print of the previous day's Regular Trading Hours at **16:14 EST** (on RTH chart setting).
  * **Opening Price:** First print of the current day's RTH session at **09:30 AM EST**.
* **Classification:**
  * **Discount ORG:** 09:30 AM Open prints **below** the 16:14 EST Settlement price.
  * **Premium ORG:** 09:30 AM Open prints **above** the 16:14 EST Settlement price.

### Consequent Encroachment (C.E.) & Standard Deviation Projections
* **50% Midpoint Delivery:** ~70% probability that price fills at least 50% (Consequent Encroachment) of the ORG by 10:00 AM EST.
* **Standard Deviation Projections (-1.0 / +1.0 SD):**
  * Measure the distance (in handles) between the 16:14 EST Settlement and the 09:30 AM Open.
  * Project **-1.0 SD** below or **+1.0 SD** above the ORG.
  * These mathematical projections frequently hit the exact high or low of the morning session to the tick.

### RTH Relative Equal Highs / Lows (Previous Market-On-Close Macro)
* Examine the last 15–20 minutes of trading from the previous day's RTH session (15:45–16:00 EST).
* Relative equal highs or lows created during the previous market close remain valid target pools during RTH, even if electronic trading (ETH / Globex overnight) traded through them.

## 7. Pillar 3: 10:00 AM Silver Bullet & First Presented FVG Rules

### The 10:00 AM Benchmark Line
* Annotate 10:00 AM EST on the 1-minute chart (avoid full vertical lines that obscure candlestick bodies).
* Observe whether the 10:00 AM candle itself forms an imbalance.

### First Presented FVG vs. First Presented FVG with Displacement
1. **Simple First Presented FVG:**
   * The absolute first Fair Value Gap (BISI or SIBI) that forms at or immediately after 10:00 AM EST.
2. **First Presented FVG WITH DISPLACEMENT (Higher Priority):**
   * Formed when an energetic up-close or down-close candle displays displacement, breaking prior short-term highs/lows and leaving an obvious gap.
   * **Hierarchical Rule:** A First Presented FVG *with Displacement* **trumps** a simple initial FVG without displacement. It commands price delivery with significantly higher authority.

### Micro Order Flow & Entry Rules
* **Premium / Discount Sensitivity:**
  * In a bearish SIBI, ideal short entry sensitivity lies in the **lower half** (between Consequent Encroachment and the bottom of the gap).
  * In a bullish BISI, ideal long entry sensitivity lies in the **upper half** (between C.E. and the top of the gap).
* **Candle Body vs. Wick Rule:**
  * **Wicks** are permitted to reach Consequent Encroachment (CE) or penetrate deeper to rebalance volume imbalances.
  * **Candle Bodies** must respect the midpoint and close within the favorable half to maintain valid order flow.
* **Stop-Loss Placement:**
  * Place stop-loss **1 tick above/below** the high/low of the candle structure that initiated the imbalance (including any adjacent volume imbalances or open/close gaps).

## 8. Narrative Integration & The Lunch Macro Draw

### Blending Narrative Components (The "Why")
Trading is not taking standalone signals; it requires combining structural evidence into a coherent narrative:
1. **Context:** Pre-Market (07:00–09:00 AM EST) creates relative equal lows at `29,370.50`.
2. **Setup:** At 10:00 AM EST, price creates a 10:00 AM High, then displays a First Presented SIBI *with Displacement*.
3. **Execution:** Short entry inside the lower half of the SIBI (at or 1 tick below C.E.). Stop-loss placed 1 tick above the swing high candle.
4. **Target (The Draw on Liquidity):**
   * **Partial 1 (Low-Hanging Fruit):** The low created during the 10:00 AM hour.
   * **Partial 2 (Lunch Macro Target 11:30–13:30):** The 07:00–09:00 AM Pre-Market Relative Equal Lows.

### Measuring Drawdown & "Heat"
* In backtesting logs, record the exact **time duration (in minutes)** and **distance (in handles/points)** that price spent in drawdown before moving into profit.
* Tracking "heat" conditions the trader to handle live trade float without panicking.
